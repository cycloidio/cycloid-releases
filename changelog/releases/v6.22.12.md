# [v6.22.12] _2026-10-07_

*This release hardens organization authentication and account registration controls.*

## 🐛 Fixes

* **Authentication:** Organization updates now preserve enabled login methods, MFA, dedicated authentication, and other settings when fields are omitted or unchanged. [ENG-437](https://linear.app/cycloid/issue/ENG-437/fixauth-keep-authentication-methods-when-saving-organization-settings) [ENG-440](https://linear.app/cycloid/issue/ENG-440/fixorgs-keep-organization-settings-that-an-update-does-not-send)
* **Account registration:** Invite-only organizations now reject account creation without a valid invitation, while invited signup continues to work. [ENG-436](https://linear.app/cycloid/issue/ENG-436/fixauth-harden-account-registration-on-invite-only-organizations)
